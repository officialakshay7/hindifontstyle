Hindi Text to Image — What It's For

This tool converts your Hindi text into a downloadable PNG image with your chosen font, size, and background colour. Perfect for creating WhatsApp DP images, Instagram posts, YouTube thumbnail text, or any graphic that needs Hindi Devanagari text without using Photoshop or Canva.

What formats can I download?

PNG with your chosen background colour, or transparent PNG (no background) for overlaying on other images in Canva, Photoshop, or CapCut.

How do I use this for YouTube thumbnails?

Type your thumbnail text in Hindi, choose a bold font (Modak or Teko work best), set a contrasting colour, download transparent PNG, then import into Canva or Photoshop over your thumbnail background.

Can I use these images on WhatsApp?

Yes — download the PNG and send it directly in WhatsApp as an image. Or set it as your DP by saving to your phone gallery first.

How to use it

  1. Type your Hindi text (or paste it from Hindi Typing).
  2. Choose a font, size, text colour and background.
  3. Download as PNG, or as a transparent PNG to place over a photo.
  4. Open the PNG in Canva, CapCut, Photoshop or WhatsApp.

Picking a font for the job

A PNG keeps the exact font and colours on every device — unlike copied text, which takes the font of the app you paste into.
