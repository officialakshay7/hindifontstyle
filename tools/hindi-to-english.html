Hindi to English Translation — Free Online

Translate Hindi text to English instantly with this free online tool. Type or paste Hindi Devanagari text and get an English translation in seconds. Works for words, sentences, and paragraphs. Also supports English to Hindi translation using the Swap button.

Is this Hindi to English translation accurate?

The translation engine is powered by Google Translate, which provides high quality Hindi-English translation. For formal or professional translation (legal, medical), always have a human translator review the output.

Can I translate English to Hindi too?

Yes — click the Swap button to switch directions. Type English in the left box and get Hindi in the right box.

Is there a word limit for translation?

The free API works best for text up to about 500 words. For longer documents, translate in sections. For full document translation, Google Translate's website handles any length.

How to use it

  1. Paste or type Hindi (Devanagari) text in the left box.
  2. The English translation appears automatically; click Translate to refresh it.
  3. Use Swap to translate English to Hindi instead.
  4. Copy the result or click the speaker to hear it.

When to trust machine translation

Machine translation is good for understanding the meaning of messages, articles and social posts. It is less reliable for idioms, poetry, names, and legal or medical wording — have a person check anything official.

Hinglish (Hindi written in English letters) usually translates poorly. Convert it to Devanagari first with Hindi Typing, then translate.

Privacy: the text you translate is sent to Google Translate to produce the result. Do not paste anything confidential.

More questions

Why did the translation stop working?

The tool relies on Google's free translation service, which can rate-limit heavy use. Wait a minute and try again, or use translate.google.com for long documents.
