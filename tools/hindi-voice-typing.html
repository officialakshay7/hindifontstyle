Hindi Voice Typing — Speak and Get Text

This Hindi voice typing tool uses your browser's built-in speech recognition to convert spoken Hindi into Devanagari text. No app installation, no API key, no cost — it works entirely in your browser using the Web Speech API available in Chrome.

Speak naturally in Hindi and the text appears in real time. You can also mix Hindi and English in the same sentence. After transcription, copy the text to use anywhere, or click "Style It" to see it in 100+ Hindi font styles.

Which browser works best for Hindi voice typing?

Google Chrome on desktop or Android. Chrome has the best Hindi (hi-IN) speech recognition support. Firefox and Safari do not support the Web Speech API fully.

Is my voice data saved anywhere?

This site never records or stores your voice. Your browser does the recognition: in Chrome, the audio is sent to Google's speech service to be transcribed, under Google's privacy policy.

Can I use this for dictating long documents?

Yes — the continuous mode keeps listening until you press stop. You can dictate paragraphs and the text accumulates in the output box. Copy it to Word or Google Docs when done.

How to use it

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome (desktop or Android).
  2. Click the microphone and allow microphone access when the browser asks.
  3. Speak in Hindi at a normal pace; pause briefly between sentences.
  4. Click again to stop, correct any words in the box, then copy.

Getting accurate results

  • Use a quiet room and keep the microphone close; earphone mics work better than laptop mics.
  • Speak whole phrases rather than single words — recognition uses context.
  • Punctuation is not added automatically; add । and commas when you edit.
  • Mixed Hindi-English is supported, but English words may come out in Devanagari spelling.
  • If the mic button does nothing, check the browser's site settings: microphone must be set to Allow.

More questions

Does this work in Safari or Firefox?

Support varies by browser and version. Chrome on desktop and Android works most reliably; if your browser has no speech recognition, the page will tell you.

Can I dictate in Marathi or other languages?

This page is set to Hindi (hi-IN). Marathi words spoken in a Hindi sentence are often recognised, but accuracy is best for Hindi.
