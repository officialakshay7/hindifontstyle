Hindi Lorem Ipsum Generator — Free Devanagari Dummy Text

Generate realistic Hindi dummy text for website design and app development. Real Devanagari words, adjustable length. Free and instant.

What is Hindi Lorem Ipsum?

Hindi Lorem Ipsum is dummy placeholder text in Devanagari script — used by designers and developers building Hindi websites, apps, and print layouts who need realistic-looking Hindi text before actual content is ready. Using this tool ensures your layouts look authentic with proper Devanagari text rather than repeated single words or English Lorem Ipsum.

When should I use Hindi dummy text instead of Lorem Ipsum?

Any time you're designing a Hindi-language interface, website, or document. English Lorem Ipsum in a Hindi layout makes it impossible to judge spacing, line breaks, and typography because Devanagari characters have different proportions and vertical rhythm.

Is this text readable Hindi or just random characters?

It's real Hindi words arranged randomly — not meaningful prose, but genuine Devanagari vocabulary. It looks authentic in a design and properly tests how your typography handles real Hindi word lengths and conjuncts.

How to use it

  1. Choose the number of paragraphs and words per paragraph.
  2. Pick classic लोरेम इप्सम or random Hindi words.
  3. Generate and copy into your design or code.
  4. Preview it in different fonts with the font style generator.

What to test with Hindi dummy text

  • Line height: Devanagari has marks above the headline (ि ी े ै ं) and below the baseline (ु ू ृ); tight line heights clip them.
  • Conjuncts: check clusters like क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र द्ध render as single shapes in your font.
  • Width: Hindi text is often wider than the same English, so buttons and menus need room.
  • Font fallback: if some letters look different, your font lacks Devanagari and the browser is substituting one.

More questions

Is the text meaningful?

No. It is real Hindi words in random order, so it looks authentic without distracting reviewers with meaning.
