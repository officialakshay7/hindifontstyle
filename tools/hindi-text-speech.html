Hindi Text to Speech — Free Online

This free Hindi text to speech tool uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis to read Hindi text aloud. It works without any API key or subscription — the Web Speech API available in Chrome and Edge handles the voice generation entirely on your device.

Use it to check pronunciation of Hindi words, create audio previews of Hindi content, help with Hindi learning, or assist users with reading difficulties.

Is this Hindi text to speech tool free?

Yes, completely free. It uses your browser's built-in Web Speech API — no sign-up, no API key, no cost per character.

Can I download the audio as an MP3?

Browser-based TTS does not support audio download directly. For downloadable Hindi audio, you would need a cloud TTS service like Google Cloud TTS or Amazon Polly (both paid).

The voice sounds robotic — how can I improve it?

Choose a different voice from the Voice dropdown — voices vary significantly by browser and device. On Android Chrome, Google's Hindi voices sound much more natural. On desktop, Microsoft's "Heera" and "Hemant" voices (if installed) also sound good.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste Hindi text.
  2. Pick a Hindi voice from the Voice list (the list depends on your device).
  3. Adjust speed and pitch, then press Play.
  4. Use Pause and Stop to control playback.

Which Hindi voices you will see

The voices come from your device and browser, not from this site. Typical Hindi (hi-IN) voices include Google हिन्दी in Chrome, Microsoft Hemant and Kalpana on Windows, and Lekha on Apple devices. If no Hindi voice is listed, install one in your system's speech or text-to-speech settings, then reload the page.

Some voices (such as Google voices in Chrome) are processed online, so your text may be sent to the voice provider. Built-in system voices work offline.

More questions

Why is the voice reading Hindi with an English accent?

An English voice is selected. Choose a voice marked Hindi or hi-IN in the Voice list.
