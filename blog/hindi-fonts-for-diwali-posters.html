Best Hindi Fonts for Diwali Posters & Greetings

Diwali designs need fonts that feel celebratory without looking cheap. These are the ones that actually work — for posters, WhatsApp greetings, and social media.

7 min read·2026-09-05

Every year around October and November, designers, small business owners, and regular people all start looking for good Hindi fonts for their Diwali content. The problem is that most Diwali-related font suggestions online are either very obvious choices everyone already uses, or decorative fonts that look great on screen but fall apart when printed or shared on WhatsApp.

This guide is based on what actually works — fonts I've tested in real poster designs, WhatsApp stickers, and social media graphics for Diwali content.

What Makes a Good Diwali Font

Diwali designs have a specific visual language — warm colours, gold and deep red palettes, a sense of celebration and tradition. Your font needs to fit that language without looking generic. Here's what to look for:

Visual warmth. Geometric, cold-looking sans-serif fonts feel out of place on Diwali content. You want something with a slightly organic quality — either a calligraphic script, a rounded display font, or a high-contrast serif that reads as traditional.

Legibility at multiple sizes. Diwali posters often have the main heading large and the details (date, venue, brand name) small. The font needs to work across both size ranges.

Print compatibility. If you're printing invitations or posters, you need a font with enough weight to hold up at 300 DPI on coloured card stock. Thin or very decorative fonts often become illegible when printed.

Top Font Picks for Diwali Content

Modak — for the main headline

Modak is the single best display font for festive Devanagari content. Its inflated, rounded letterforms look joyful and celebratory — exactly the energy Diwali content needs. It photographs well, renders beautifully on screen, and survives WhatsApp's JPEG compression without losing clarity. Use it for your main heading: दीपावली की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएं, or your brand name in a poster headline. Download free →

Yatra One — for a traditional touch

Yatra One has a slightly more structured, traditional quality compared to Modak. It's a display serif that feels rooted in Indian design heritage — you'll see it used in temple trust announcements, religious event posters, and traditional newspaper layouts. For a Diwali poster that needs to feel respectful and traditional rather than playful, Yatra One is the better pick. Download free →

Baloo 2 Bold — for modern Diwali content

If you're creating Diwali content for a modern brand — an e-commerce sale banner, a restaurant's Diwali menu, a startup's social media post — Baloo 2 at Bold or ExtraBold weight strikes the right balance. It's festive without being so traditional that it looks out of place on a modern design. Comes in five weights, so you can use Bold for the headline and Regular for supporting text. Download free →

Amita — for Diwali greeting cards

For more intimate content — a personal Diwali greeting card, a WhatsApp message with a special design, a wedding-adjacent event during the Diwali season — Amita's calligraphic warmth works beautifully. It reads as hand-crafted and personal in a way that display fonts can't replicate. Download free →

Pairing Fonts for Diwali Designs

Most good Diwali designs use two fonts — one for the main heading and one for body text or supporting details. Here are combinations that work well:

  • Modak + Hind: Festive headline + clean body. Best for social media posts and banners.
  • Yatra One + Noto Sans Devanagari: Traditional heading + neutral body. Best for event posters and printed invitations.
  • Amita Bold + Laila: Calligraphic heading + elegant body. Best for greeting cards and personal content.
  • Baloo 2 ExtraBold + Baloo 2 Regular: Using the same font family at different weights. Simplest approach, always looks coherent.

Colour Combinations That Work With These Fonts

The font alone doesn't make a Diwali design — the colour combination matters as much. Some combinations that consistently perform well:

  • Deep maroon background + gold text — classic, premium, traditional
  • Dark navy + warm yellow — modern take on festive, works for tech brands
  • Saffron/orange background + white + dark brown text — bright, vibrant, social-media ready
  • Black background + gold Modak headline + white body text — dramatic, works well for sale banners

For WhatsApp Diwali Stickers and Greetings

WhatsApp stickers and greeting images have specific constraints — they're viewed on small screens at low resolution. For this use case, go with the boldest, thickest option available. Modak is the clear winner here. Keep your text short — शुभ दीपावली is enough. Don't try to fit more than 4–5 words in a WhatsApp sticker.
