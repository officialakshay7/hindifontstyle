Hindi Calligraphy Fonts — 15 Best Free Downloads for 2026

The most beautiful handwriting and calligraphy fonts for Devanagari — with live previews and free download links for each.

8 min read·Published March 5, 2026·Updated September 13, 2026·By Akshay

Calligraphy fonts carry an emotional weight that printed or digital text often lacks. When you see a wedding invitation written in flowing Devanagari calligraphy, it signals care, beauty, and intention long before you read the words. Here are the 15 most beautiful Hindi calligraphy and handwriting fonts — all free from Google Fonts, tested at real design sizes, and genuinely useful rather than just decorative.

What Makes a Good Hindi Calligraphy Font?

A great Devanagari calligraphy font balances three things: authentic handwritten character, sufficient legibility for reading, and good conjunct consonant handling. Conjuncts — where two consonants join into a single merged glyph, like क्ष or त्र — are the hardest part of Devanagari type design. A font that handles conjuncts poorly produces cramped, awkward letterforms that look wrong to fluent Hindi readers even if they cannot articulate exactly what's off. Every font on this list handles conjuncts correctly.

The 15 Best Hindi Calligraphy Fonts

1. Kalam — Best Handwriting Font for Social Media

The most loved Devanagari handwriting font. Kalam genuinely looks hand-penned without sacrificing legibility — a balance most calligraphy fonts fail at. Available in Light, Regular, and Bold weights. Works across a surprisingly wide range of contexts. At display sizes the handwritten quality is expressive and personal. At smaller sizes (12pt+) it stays readable in a way most calligraphy fonts don't. Best for: Instagram quotes, WhatsApp status, educational worksheets, casual social posts. Download free →

2. Amita — Best for Wedding Invitations

Graceful and flowing, Amita is inspired by traditional Indian calligraphic styles. It reads as refined and intentional — formal in a way Kalam isn't. The Bold weight is particularly striking for recipient names on wedding invitations and certificates. It handles the complex letterforms in common wedding vocabulary (शुभ, विवाह, निमंत्रण) with exceptional elegance. Best for: wedding cards, certificates, event branding, formal invitations. Download free →

3. Tillana — Best for Cultural Content

Named after the Indian classical composition form, Tillana has dance-like letterforms. Five weights give it real flexibility — Thin and Light work for understated elegance, Bold and ExtraBold for maximum impact. It's refined enough for formal use but expressive enough for artistic projects. Best for: classical arts, cultural institutions, music and dance content, Diwali and festival posters. Download free →

4. Ranga — Best Theatrical Calligraphy

Theatrical and bold, Ranga combines calligraphic tradition with dramatic presence. It has more visual weight than Amita or Kalam, making it better for headlines that need to hold their own against strong background images. Best for: entertainment posters, performance arts, decorative headlines, aggressive festival content. Download free →

5. Dekko — Best Casual Calligraphy

Dekko balances a casual handwritten feel with good screen readability. Unlike most calligraphy fonts that struggle at body text sizes, Dekko works at 14–16pt on screen — a rare quality. Best for: educational content, children's apps, informal social media where you want warmth without formality. Download free →

6. Laila — Best Formal Calligraphy

An elegant formal script with classical proportions. Laila excels where you need calligraphic beauty without sacrificing formality. Five weights including a particularly useful SemiBold. Best for: certificates, awards, official documents with character, institutional branding. Download free →

7. Sahitya — Best for Literary Content

Sahitya (meaning "literature" in Sanskrit) is a serif with calligraphic qualities designed for extended reading. Best for: books, academic papers, literary publications, long-form articles where reading comfort matters as much as visual personality. Download free →

8. Karma — Traditional Newspaper Calligraphy

Karma has strong vertical strokes and clear horizontal headers, giving it a formal newspaper-like quality while still carrying the warmth of handcrafted letterforms. Five weights. Best for: editorial content, traditional institutions, newspaper-style layouts. Download free →

9. Gotu — Minimalist Calligraphy

At display sizes, Gotu's proportions have a quiet elegance that rewards attention. It's the right calligraphy font when the design should feel restrained and sophisticated rather than expressive. Best for: premium brands, minimalist design, luxury packaging, high-end event stationery. Download free →

10. Martel — Best Editorial Calligraphy

Martel combines calligraphic origins with a clean editorial sensibility. Eight weights make it the most versatile calligraphy-adjacent font on Google Fonts. Particularly good for bilingual layouts where Hindi needs to match the weight and personality of an English serif. Best for: magazines, editorial design, premium content sites, bilingual publications. Download free →

11. Tiro Devanagari Hindi — Scholar's Choice

Designed by a type scholar with deep knowledge of Devanagari calligraphic tradition, Tiro has exceptional conjunct consonant coverage — more than almost any other free Devanagari font. Best for: academic publishing, legal documents, government materials. Download free →

12. Inknut Antiqua — Antique Calligraphy

Inknut Antiqua has a letterpress quality — as if the letters were pressed into paper with calligraphic metal type. Its deliberate irregularities are features, not flaws. Best for: heritage brands, artisan businesses, book covers, traditional craft businesses. Download free →

13. Vesper Libre — Manuscript Calligraphy

Vesper Libre has a scholarly, manuscript quality designed for high-quality book printing. At display sizes it reads as traditional and learned. Best for: academic content, historical topics, religious text. Download free →

14. Asar — Heritage Calligraphy

Asar's letterforms have a handcrafted quality that feels genuinely historical. Best for situations where calligraphy should reference India's manuscript heritage directly. Best for: heritage brands, cultural institutions, historical content. Download free →

15. Eczar — Classical Display Calligraphy

A display serif with calligraphic proportions — high contrast between thick and thin strokes, elegant curves, classical personality. Six weights. Particularly good when you need a calligraphic font that also pairs well with Latin typography. Best for: book covers, editorial headlines, brand names. Download free →

Which Hindi Calligraphy Font Should You Choose?

  • Wedding invitations: Amita Bold for names, Laila Regular for details
  • Instagram and WhatsApp quotes: Kalam Bold
  • Diwali and festival posters: Tillana ExtraBold or Ranga Bold
  • Certificates and awards: Laila SemiBold for the name, Martel Regular for body
  • Children's educational materials: Dekko
  • Premium brand identity: Amita or Gotu
  • Books and academic content: Sahitya, Vesper Libre, or Tiro Devanagari

How to Download Hindi Calligraphy Fonts Free

  1. Click any download link above, or go to fonts.google.com
  2. Search the font name and click "Download family"
  3. Extract the ZIP to get .ttf files
  4. Windows: right-click .ttf → Install. Mac: double-click → Install Font
  5. The font appears immediately in Word, Photoshop, Canva desktop, and all other apps

Using Hindi Calligraphy Fonts on Canva

Canva includes several Hindi calligraphy fonts built in. Search "Kalam", "Amita", or "Tillana" in Canva's font dropdown — they load directly. For fonts not in Canva's library, Canva Pro lets you upload custom .ttf files under Brand Kit → Brand Fonts → Upload a font.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best Hindi calligraphy font for wedding cards?

Amita Bold is the best choice. Its flowing letterforms feel genuinely celebratory. Use it for the couple's names and the main heading. For body text (date, venue), use Laila Regular for readability at smaller sizes.

Are Hindi calligraphy fonts free for commercial use?

Yes — all fonts listed here are Google Fonts under OFL (Open Font License). Free for personal and commercial use including client work, print, and digital content. No attribution required.

How do I use Hindi calligraphy fonts on WhatsApp?

What is the difference between a calligraphy and regular Hindi font?

Regular Hindi fonts (Noto Sans Devanagari, Hind) are designed for maximum readability in extended text. Calligraphy fonts (Amita, Kalam) are designed to evoke handwriting — more expressive but less suited for long paragraphs. Use calligraphy for headlines and names; regular fonts for body text.
