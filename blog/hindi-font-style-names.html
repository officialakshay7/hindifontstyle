Hindi Font Style Names: 61 Devanagari Fonts to Try

A practical directory of Hindi font families, what their styles look like, and how to pick one for reading, social posts, or design.

7 min read·Published November 5, 2026·By Akshay

Looking for a Hindi font style name to use in a post, heading, invitation, or document? Start by choosing the kind of letterform you need: a clear font for reading, a decorative display font for a few large words, or a handwritten style for a personal touch. This guide groups the 61 Devanagari font families in our Hindi font style generator so you can compare them by purpose.

One useful distinction: a font family is the typeface itself, while a font style can also mean a weight or visual treatment. The generator includes 149 weight variants and additional effects, for 195+ previews in total. The names below are the families; each can look different depending on its weight, size, and the text you enter.

Hindi font styles by category

These are the family names available in the live collection. Category labels are a starting point, not strict rules: preview the exact Hindi word you plan to use because letter shapes and spacing can feel different from one word to another.

Sans serif: clear, versatile letterforms

Sans serif families are a practical first choice for interface text, captions, and general-purpose layouts. The collection includes Gotu, Noto Sans Devanagari, Poppins, Arya, Hind, Palanquin Dark, Mukta, Anek Devanagari, Yantramanav, Cambay, Khula, Sarala, Halant, Rajdhani, Khand, Pragati Narrow, Jaldi, Akshar, Amiko, Palanquin, Google Sans, IBM Plex Sans Devanagari, Martel Sans, Matangi, and Sarpanch.

Serif: a more traditional or editorial tone

Serif families can add a formal, literary, or print-inspired character to titles and selected passages. Try Glegoo, Inknut Antiqua, Tiro Devanagari Hindi, Karma, Martel, Sahitya, Eczar, Sura, Asar, Kurale, Sumana, Vesper Libre, Kadwa, Rhodium Libre, Noto Serif Devanagari, Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit, Tiro Devanagari Marathi, and Annapurna SIL.

Display: distinctive lettering for short text

Display fonts are designed to draw attention. They are often most useful for a few words in a poster, thumbnail, greeting, or logo concept—not dense paragraphs. Browse Modak, Teko, Baloo 2, Jaini Purva, Rozha One, Yatra One, Biryani, Alkatra, Bakbak One, Gajraj One, and Jaini.

Calligraphy and handwritten styles

For a more personal or expressive appearance, compare Kalam, Tillana, Amita, Laila, Ranga, Dekko, and Playpen Sans Deva. A handwritten face can work well for a short greeting or invitation heading, but check that every character in your text is clear at the final size.

How to choose the right Hindi font style

  1. Start with the purpose. Use a readable family for paragraphs and a more distinctive display family for short headings.
  2. Preview your actual text. Enter your Hindi phrase, name, or title; a sample word may not show how your own conjuncts and vowel marks will look.
  3. Check size and contrast. Thin strokes can disappear when reduced. Zoom out to the size people will actually see.
  4. Limit the number of families. One text family plus one heading family is usually enough to create hierarchy without making a design feel crowded.
  5. Test on the destination. A font used in an image or design file is different from text pasted into a social profile. Check the result in the app and on a second device where possible.

Font name, font style, and copy-paste text are different

A font family such as Kalam or Noto Sans Devanagari changes how Devanagari characters are drawn when the font is available in the app or design. Weight variants—such as light, regular, or bold—belong to that family. Effects and decorations change the preview further.

Copying text does not normally transfer an installed font to Instagram or WhatsApp. The copied characters remain text, and the receiving app chooses how to render them. If you need the exact appearance to stay fixed, use the generator's image export or download the font and use it in a design application. Frequently asked questions

Which Hindi font style is easiest to read?

For longer text, begin with a clean sans serif family such as Noto Sans Devanagari, Hind, Mukta, or Arya. Readability also depends on font size, line spacing, contrast, and the device.

Are these font families free to use?

The generator links to Google Fonts families. Check the licence shown on each family page before redistributing font files or using a font in a project with specific legal requirements.

Can I paste a Hindi font style into WhatsApp or Instagram?

You can paste the text characters, but the receiving app may not preserve the exact typeface or visual effects. For a fixed design, export an image or use the font in a design app. See our Hindi font download and installation guide.
