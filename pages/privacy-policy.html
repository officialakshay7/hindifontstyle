Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HindiFontStyle.co.in ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our website.

Information We Do Not Collect

HindiFontStyle.co.in is a client-side tool. The text you type into our font generator is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to our servers. We do not store, log, or transmit any text you enter.

Two exceptions use Google services: the Hindi to English tool sends the text you translate to Google Translate, and Hindi Voice Typing uses your browser's speech recognition, which in Chrome sends audio to Google to transcribe it. We do not receive or store that text or audio.

We do not require user accounts, do not collect names or email addresses through general site usage, and do not store any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily submit it via our contact form.

Information We Collect Automatically

We use standard web analytics (Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. This may collect:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Referring website or search query

This data is anonymised and aggregated. We cannot identify you personally from this information.

Cookies

We use cookies to operate core site features, understand how visitors use our site, and serve relevant advertising.

When you first visit HindiFontStyle.co.in, you'll see a cookie consent banner with three choices:

Accept All — enables all cookies, including analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense) cookies, so we can measure site usage and serve relevant ads.

Necessary Only — enables only the cookies required for the site to function correctly (such as remembering your consent choice). Google Analytics runs in Consent Mode without analytics cookies, and Google AdSense shows non-personalised ads only.

Decline — no optional cookies are set. Only strictly necessary cookies (such as the one that remembers you declined) are used. As with Necessary Only, ads are non-personalised and analytics runs without cookies.

Until you make a choice, the site behaves as if you chose Necessary Only. Non-personalised ads may still use cookies for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention, as described in Google's advertising policy.

Your choice is stored in your browser and remembered for future visits. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's site data for hindifontstyle.co.in, which will cause the banner to reappear on your next visit.

You can also disable cookies entirely through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect the core functionality of our font generator, converter tools, or any text processing — these run entirely in your browser and never depend on cookies.

Google Fonts

Our tool loads fonts from Google Fonts via their API. When fonts are loaded, Google may collect your IP address and browser information as part of serving the font files. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out via Google Ad Settings.

Contact Form

If you submit a message via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not share this information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us immediately.

Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data we hold about you (limited to contact form submissions). To exercise this right, contact us at contact@hindifontstyle.co.in.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at contact@hindifontstyle.co.in.
